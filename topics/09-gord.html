🔥 GORD / Dyspepsia

NSW permanent service · Patient ≥18 AND ≤50 years · Up to 4 weeks supply · NSW Approved Medicines dated 25 February 2026
💡 GORD = frequent (≥2 episodes/week) heartburn or regurgitation severe enough to impair quality of life, OR with complications. >50 yo with new symptoms = REFER (red flag for malignancy). Lifestyle first, then antacid/alginate, then H2RA, then PPI.

✅ Eligibility

  • Patient ≥18 AND ≤50 years
  • Classic GORD: heartburn, regurgitation (atypical: belching, epigastric pain, dyspepsia, acid brash)
  • No RED flags below

💊 Stepwise management

StepAction
1 — Lifestyle (always)Weight loss if BMI ↑ (single most effective) · elevate head of bed 15–20 cm (blocks not pillows) · avoid lying within 3h of meals · smaller meals · avoid triggers (alcohol, caffeine, fatty/spicy, chocolate, peppermint, citrus) · smoking cessation · avoid tight clothing · sleep on left side
2 — Antacids / alginates PRN (mild intermittent)Calcium carbonate (Quick-Eze) · Al/Mg hydroxide (Mylanta — Al constipates, Mg causes diarrhoea) · Alginate (Gaviscon) — raft on stomach acid · separate from other meds by 2h (chelation: tetracyclines, FQ, levothyroxine, iron, bisphosphonates)
3 — H2RAFamotidine 20 mg PO BD; onset 30–60 min; good for nocturnal sx; tachyphylaxis at 2–6 wk → step up if persistent
4 — PPI first-line for typical GORDTake 30 min before breakfast (proton pumps activated by food). 4–8 week course initially, then step down to lowest effective dose or PRN. Review at 4 weeks — no improvement → refer.

📋 Long-term PPI counselling

  • Step down or use PRN preferred
  • Long-term risks (modest individual): B12, magnesium, iron deficiency; fracture risk (DEXA if risk factors); C. difficile; CKD slight ↑ risk
  • Rebound hypersecretion on stopping abruptly — taper over 2–4 weeks if on >8 weeks

👶 Pregnancy

  • Antacids + H2RA generally safe at standard doses
  • PPIs TGA Category B3 most · rabeprazole is B1
  • If PPI required: omeprazole has most clinical experience

🗣️ Counselling (must cover)

  • Lifestyle first — esp. weight, head-of-bed, no late meals
  • PPI 30 min before breakfast — narrate this (students lose marks for "with food")
  • Antacid spacing 2h from other meds
  • Review at 4 weeks; step down or PRN once controlled
  • Return if any ALARMS feature develops

📋 Documentation

SOAP record per NSW Authority. PPI-clopidogrel check documented if relevant. Step-down plan. Share with GP within 48h; retain ≥7 years.

🎯 OSCE pearls

  • Age ≥18 AND ≤50 stated explicitly (>50 = refer)
  • ALARMS screen verbalised
  • Cardiac differential acknowledged (chest pain → ED)
  • Medication review (causative drugs)
  • Lifestyle counsel first
  • PPI dosing: 30 min before breakfast
  • Step-down plan + 4-week review mentioned
  • 4-week supply maximum

⚠️ Pitfalls

  • 54 yo with chest burning + nausea + SOB + smoking + HTN + T2DM → ED (rule out MI); also >50 = ineligible
  • 45 yo on clopidogrel → rabeprazole (NOT omeprazole/esomeprazole — interaction)
  • Pregnancy GORD: lifestyle, alginate, calcium carbonate first; omeprazole if PPI needed
  • Refer all new GORD >50 yo — could be malignancy
