💉 IM Injection (Procedures)

OSCE Station 6 · Pharmacist administration of intramuscular injections — vaccines, depot contraception, long-acting antibiotics, B12 · Technique, sites, anaphylaxis readiness + documentation
💡 IM = medication into muscle for rapid systemic absorption when the oral route is unsuitable, or for vaccination / depot therapy. The marker assesses your 5 checks + consent, correct site landmarking, technique, anaphylaxis readiness, and documentation.

✅ Before you inject

  • Confirm patient identity + indication; check medication, dose, expiry, batch number, appearance
  • Informed consent — procedure, benefits, risks, alternatives
  • Screen contraindications/precautions for the drug (e.g. exclude pregnancy for DMPA)
  • Hand hygiene · PPE · single-use equipment · aseptic technique · sharps bin ready
  • Anaphylaxis kit + adrenaline immediately available; position patient comfortably

📍 Site selection

SiteLandmarkMax volumeUse
Deltoid2–3 finger-widths below the acromion, central thickest bulk~1 mL (up to 2)Most common in pharmacy — vaccines, B12
VentroglutealPalm on greater trochanter, index to ASIS, middle finger to iliac crest → inject in the "V"up to 3 mLLarger / viscous / depot — DMPA, benzathine penicillin, cabotegravir
Vastus lateralisOuter mid-thigh2–3 mLAlternative; preferred in infants
⚠️ SIRVA (Shoulder Injury Related to Vaccine Administration) — caused by injecting too high in the deltoid near the acromion/bursa. Landmark carefully.

💉 Needle + technique

  • Needle: deltoid 23–25 G × 25 mm; ventrogluteal/vastus 21–23 G × 38 mm (longer for larger build / viscous depot)
  • Angle: insert at 90°, dart-like, into the muscle bulk
  • Z-track for irritant/staining depots — displace skin laterally, inject, withdraw, release → seals the track
  • Inject steadily; withdraw; apply gentle pressure (do not massage a depot/Z-track); dispose of sharp immediately
  • Aspiration is not routine for deltoid vaccines (Australian Immunisation Handbook)

🔴 Anaphylaxis — be ready every time

StepAction
RecogniseWheeze, stridor, swelling (lips/tongue/throat), urticaria, hypotension, collapse
Adrenaline IMInto vastus lateralis FIRST — adult 0.5 mg (0.5 mL of 1:1000); child 0.01 mg/kg (max 0.5 mg); repeat every 5 min PRN
EscalateCall 000; lie flat (sit only if breathing difficulty — do NOT stand); high-flow O₂; monitor ABC
AfterDocument + report the adverse event

📋 Documentation (every injection)

Medication + dose · batch number + expiry · injection site · route · date/time · consent · adverse events · administering pharmacist. Upload vaccines to AIR / My Health Record.

🎯 OSCE pearls (Station 6)

  • Verbalise the 5 checks (patient, drug, dose, expiry, batch) + consent before touching the patient
  • Landmark the site aloud and explain SIRVA avoidance for the deltoid
  • State needle gauge/length for the site + build; use Z-track for depots
  • Confirm anaphylaxis kit ready and state the adrenaline dose if asked
  • Aseptic technique narrated; sharps disposed immediately; document batch + site
  • DMPA → exclude pregnancy + counsel; benzathine penicillin → viscous/painful, inject slowly
