🚭 Smoking Cessation

NSW planned/pilot pharmacist service · Full OTC Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT) + counselling · S4 supply of varenicline + bupropion
💡 5 A's brief intervention: Ask · Assess · Advise · Assist · Arrange. Behavioural support + pharmacotherapy doubles success rate vs either alone. Quitline 13 78 48 is free + provides callbacks. Vaping (e-cigarettes) is currently prescription-only in NSW for therapeutic use.

✅ Eligibility

  • Any adult smoker willing to quit or reduce
  • Assess nicotine dependence using time-to-first-cigarette (surrogate for Fagerström Test for Nicotine Dependence, FTND): <30 min after waking = high; <5 min = very high
  • No RED flags below

🔴 RED — caution / specific contraindication → refer

TriggerAction
Recent MI / unstable angina / arrhythmia (within 2 weeks)NRT caution; prefer intermittent forms; refer GP
Active suicidal ideation + considering varenicline/bupropionRefer GP — NRT only, refer for behavioural support
Active eating disorder, alcohol/benzo withdrawalBupropion contraindicated → refer
Clozapine / olanzapine / theophylline users considering quittingMUST coordinate with prescriber — cessation ↓ CYP1A2 induction → drug levels ↑ → toxicity. Psychiatrist reduces dose 30–50% over 1–2 weeks.

🟠 ORANGE — TREAT + concurrently refer

TriggerAction
Severe COPD, IHD, established CVDNRT generally safe; coordinate with GP
Mental health conditionQuitting doesn't worsen MH (often improves) but monitor; refer for MH support
Pregnancy / breastfeedingRefer GP; intermittent NRT preferred (avoid patch — continuous fetal exposure); varenicline/bupropion avoid
Failed multiple quit attemptsRefer GP for combined behavioural + pharmacotherapy

💊 Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT) — first-line

Combination NRT (long-acting patch + short-acting gum/lozenge/spray) is more effective than monotherapy. Treat 8–12 weeks then taper.
FormStrength / doseKey Notes
Patch (long-acting)21 mg/24h (>10 cig/day); 14 mg (≤10 cig/day)Apply hairless skin, rotate site; remove at night if vivid dreams
Gum (short-acting)2 mg (low dep) or 4 mg (high). Chew-park technique. 1 piece q1–2h, max 24/dayNo acidic drinks 15 min before/during (lowers absorption)
Lozenge1.5–4 mg. Suck slowly, park between cheek + gumSame acidic drink caveat
Inhalator10 mg cartridgeMimics hand-mouth ritual
Mouth spray (Nicorette QuickMist)1 mg/spray, 1–2 prn, max 64/dayFastest acting
Example combination: 21 mg patch daily + 2 mg gum/lozenge PRN for cravings.

🌫️ E-cigarettes (vaping) — NSW

⛔ Prescription-only access for therapeutic use; OTC restricted (legislation changing — verify). Limited evidence in long-term cessation. Emerging cardiopulmonary risks. If patient vaping + smoking → support transition off both.

👶 Special populations

Pregnancy

  • Behavioural support first-line
  • If NRT used: intermittent forms (gum, lozenge, spray) preferred over patch (reduces continuous fetal exposure)
  • Varenicline + bupropion: avoid

Breastfeeding

  • Intermittent NRT preferred; time feed before next dose

Mental health

  • Smoking 2× more prevalent in serious MI
  • Quitting does NOT worsen MH outcomes — often improves
  • Cessation → ↓ clozapine/olanzapine clearance → ↑ levels → toxicity → liaise with psychiatry to ↓ dose 30–50%

🗣️ Counselling (must cover)

  • Set quit date within 2 weeks
  • Withdrawal peaks days 3–5, resolves over 2–4 weeks
  • Average weight gain 4–5 kg — pre-empt with exercise + diet plan
  • Relapse common — multiple attempts normal, not failure
  • Plan for triggers — routine change, alcohol limit, support network
  • Refer to Quitline 13 78 48 (free NRT available via Quitline for many in NSW)
  • Caffeine clearance halves on cessation → counsel ↓ coffee intake to avoid anxiety/insomnia

📋 Documentation

Record: smoking hx, FTND surrogate, quit date, NRT regimen, follow-up plan, MH/pregnancy screen. Share with GP if Rx supplied; retain ≥7 years.

🎯 OSCE pearls

  • 5 A's: Ask + assess + advise + assist + arrange — narrate the framework
  • Non-judgemental tone
  • Assess dependence (time-to-first-cigarette)
  • Discuss NRT options + combination
  • Mention Quitline 13 78 48 explicitly
  • Set quit date + arrange follow-up
  • Pregnancy / breastfeeding screen
  • MH screen + medication levels caveat (clozapine, olanzapine, theophylline)

⚠️ Pitfalls

  • Clozapine + cessation = toxicity — must reduce dose by 30–50% (psychiatry)
  • Caffeine clearance halves on cessation → anxiety + insomnia; counsel ↓ coffee intake
  • Behavioural support + pharmacotherapy doubles success rate vs either alone
  • Don't supply varenicline if active suicidal ideation
