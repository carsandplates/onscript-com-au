🦠 Uncomplicated UTI

NSW PATH-UTI permanent service · Non-pregnant female 18–65 yo · NSW Health Authority (19 Apr 2023 — verify current)
💡 27–42% of uncomplicated UTI cases self-resolve in 3–7 days. Watchful waiting + analgesia is a valid first option (AMS). One symptom alone ≠ UTI — needs ≥2 of dysuria / urinary frequency / urinary urgency / suprapubic pain.

✅ Eligibility (all required)

  • Female, 18–65 yo, physically present, informed consent
  • ≥2 of: dysuria, urinary frequency, urinary urgency, suprapubic pain/discomfort
  • No RED flags below

🔴 RED — DO NOT treat → refer

⛔ Pharmacist does not supply an antibiotic. Provide analgesia, hydration, urine sample jar, document, refer.

🟠 ORANGE — TREAT + concurrently refer to GP

Supply is appropriate, but document GP referral at the same consult for follow-up review.
Trigger
Multi-drug resistance (MDR) risk: antibiotic in last 3 mo · hospitalisation or aged care stay in 4 wk · overseas travel in 3 mo (6 mo if South/SE Asia) · known MDR infection in 3 mo
STI risk: age <29 or prior STI · unprotected sex outside monogamy · new partner <60 d · partner recently treated for STI · sex with sex worker
SGLT2 inhibitor user · IUD in situ ≥3 months

💊 Conservative care (always offer)

Per updated eTG: non-antibiotic management is first-line for early mild uUTI (NNT 3–6 for symptom relief at day 3).
  • Paracetamol 1 g PO q4–6h prn (max 4 g/24h)
  • Ibuprofen 200–400 mg PO q6–8h prn × 3 days (OTC ceiling 1200 mg/24h; check renal/GI/anticoagulant)
  • Fluids 2–3 L/day; void after sex; front-to-back wiping
  • Cranberry: prevention only — no evidence for acute treatment

🗣️ Counselling (must cover)

  • Collect urine sample BEFORE first dose, refrigerate, take to GP if no improvement at 48 h
  • Symptoms should improve within 48 hours of starting antibiotic
  • Nitrofurantoin + urinary alkaliniser = no effect — hold Ural / Citravescent
  • Return / ED for: fever ≥38 °C, flank pain, vomiting, rigors, persistent blood, pregnancy possibility, no improvement at 48 h
  • No GP? HealthDirect 1800 022 222

📋 Documentation

Record in MedAdvisor: patient ID, date, pharmacist + AHPRA, hx/exam, opinion, action, drug particulars, advice, consent. Share with GP within 48 h (with consent); upload to My Health Record unless declined. Retain ≥7 years (or to age 25 if patient <18).

🎯 OSCE pearls

  • State each eligibility criterion aloud
  • NSW first-line is nitrofurantoin — don't default to trimethoprim (old order)
  • Verbalise the alkaliniser interaction — high-yield easy mark
  • AMS framing: "not every eligible patient needs an antibiotic"
  • Pregnancy possibility check always
