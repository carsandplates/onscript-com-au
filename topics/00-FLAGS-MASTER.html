20 — When Not to Prescribe (active decision principle)

Non-prescribing is an active, documented decision. Triggers: (1) early/mild self-limiting disease (eTG uUTI: non-abx first-line; NNT 3–6); (2) diagnostic uncertainty; (3) outside scope (age, severity, pregnancy, exclusion); (4) safety concerns; (5) lack of clinical benefit; (6) risk of harm or misuse (AMS); (7) preventive context (UKMEC 3-4). Document with same rigour as prescribing.
