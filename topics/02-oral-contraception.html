💊 Oral Contraception (Resupply)

NSW Pharmacist Practice Standards — Hormonal Contraception Continuation (Authority 25 Feb 2026) · Now covers continuation of the oral pill, DMPA injection and combined vaginal ring · Continuation of existing therapy only · NOT for new initiation
💡 Approved substances: ethinyloestradiol (35 µg or less) · levonorgestrel · norethisterone · drospirenone · nomegestrol · desogestrel · dienogest · gestodene. Continuation (not initiation) of the patient's current contraception for up to 12 months, prescribed/supplied by a medical or nurse practitioner over the previous 24 months. Cannot initiate or change therapy. Verify against the UK Medical Eligibility Criteria for Contraceptive Use (UKMEC) — category 3/4 (e.g. migraine with aura) is not eligible.
📋 Three categories in scope (previously oral-only — DMPA injection and vaginal ring are new):

Injectable medroxyprogesterone (DMPA) 150 mg/mL (Depo-Provera, Depo-Ralovera)Female 25–39 yrsAdminister only if completed PSA Administering Medicines by Injection course — else refer

BP measured + recorded every 12 months; BMI at first presentation. Must not supply if >12 months of continuation already given under this authority. Records ≥7 years; share with GP within 1 week with consent.

✅ Eligibility (resupply)

  • Adult on stable existing OCP (used >2 years, regular GP review documented)
  • OCP on NSW-approved list (see above)
  • BP measured this visit (<140/90); HR + BMI taken; no new symptoms
  • No RED flags below

🔴 RED — STOP pill / DO NOT supply

⛔ Acute symptoms = ED. Failed eligibility = refer GP (no resupply).

💊 Missed-pill rules — counsel verbatim

Combined Oral Contraceptive (COC)

  • Missed 1 pill (<48h late): take ASAP, continue pack, no extra precautions
  • Missed ≥2 pills (>48h late): take most recent missed pill; condoms 7 days; if week 1 → consider emergency contraception; if week 3 → skip placebo, start new pack immediately

🗣️ Counselling (must cover)

  • BP measurement narrated + value stated aloud
  • Active screen: migraine with aura, smoking, VTE risk, calf inspection
  • Missed-pill rules in patient-friendly language
  • Most contraceptives don't protect against STIs — only condoms do; promote dual protection
  • Annual GP review minimum
  • Amoxicillin no longer thought to reduce OCP efficacy unless causes vomiting/diarrhoea

📋 Documentation

Record: BP, HR, BMI, current pill name + duration, next GP review date, advice given, consent. Share with GP; retain ≥7 years.

🎯 OSCE pearls

  • BP measurement narrated + value stated aloud
  • Verbalise UKMEC framework
  • Missed-pill rules in plain language
  • Confirm last GP review date
  • 1800RESPECT / 13YARN if coercion disclosed
  • Don't initiate new COC under the continuation authority — refer GP for new starts (continuation only)
