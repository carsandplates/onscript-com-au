❤️ Cardiovascular + Respiratory Examination

Foundation skill across NSW PATH services · BP for OCP resupply · Cardiac differential for GORD · Red flag screen for MSK · Vital signs for vaccinations + smoking cessation · Future asthma/COPD/CV risk services
💡 Traditional examination sequence: Inspection → Palpation → Percussion → Auscultation. (Exception: abdomen — auscultate BEFORE percussion + palpation; otherwise you alter bowel sounds.)

👁️ Initial observations (preliminary clues)

  • Looks sick? Breathless? In pain?
  • Posture (kyphosis, scoliosis)
  • Build (malnourished / thin / normal / overweight / obese)
  • Mobility, gait, mobility aid
  • Expression (happy / depressed / confident / anxious / agitated)
  • Tremor; face pallor / flushed; colour (jaundice, rash); dress + grooming

BP measurement technique

  • Standard cuff width = 12.5 cm; bigger cuff if large arm (too small → over-estimation)
  • Korotkoff: phase 1 = SBP, phase 5 = DBP
  • Patient seated 5 min rest; arm at heart level; no caffeine/smoking 30 min

Pulse measurement

  • Radial (rate, rhythm, character, volume)
  • Feel for ≥30 sec for rate
  • Rhythm: regular / regularly irregular (sinus arrhythmia) / chaotic (AF)
  • Comparisons: radial-radial, radial-femoral (delay = coarctation)

Pulse oximeter technique

  • Clip on index or middle finger
  • Remove nail polish, fake nails; warm hands; sit still
  • Rest 5 min; allow instrument to equilibrate >5 sec
  • <92% → seek medical advice
  • <88% → 🔴 RED FLAG → ED

❤️ Cardiac examination

Praecordium — palpation + auscultation

  • Cardiac apex beat: 5th intercostal space, 1 cm medial to left midclavicular line
  • Displaced apex → cardiomegaly

Carotids — palpation + auscultation

  • Reveal character of pulse
  • Thrill (palpable loud murmur)
  • Bruit (rough-sounding murmur over artery) → partial obstruction, usually atherosclerosis → REFER
  • NEVER palpate both carotids at once

🦵 Periphery — lower limbs

Inspection

  • Colour (normal / white / blue / red / congested / blotchy)
  • Swelling (unilateral / bilateral / symmetrical)
  • Lumps · veins (varicosities, thrombophlebitis, deep thrombosis) · ulcers

Palpation

  • Temperature (especially distally)
  • Swelling, tenderness

Swollen ankles workup

  • Unilateral vs bilateral?
  • Extent of swelling?
  • Pitting? Press gently with thumb for 15 sec
  • RV failure: sluggish venous return → poor capillary fluid pickup in dependent areas → bilateral pitting oedema
  • Unilateral calf tenderness without trauma → DVT until proven otherwise

🫁 Respiratory examination

Anatomy key points

  • Right lung 3 lobes, left lung 2 lobes
  • Right main bronchus shorter + more vertical (foreign body → right)
  • Trachea midline or very slightly right at sternal notch
  • Lungs surrounded by visceral + parietal pleura

Chest inspection

  • Form (kypho-scoliosis, barrel chest = hyperinflation/COPD)
  • Squared/raised shoulders (hyperinflation)
  • Posture: sitting forward with hands on knees (COPD tripod)
  • RR (normal 12–20); use of accessory muscles
  • Cough, sputum, hoarse voice
  • Noisy breathing (wheeze, stridor)
  • Pulsus paradoxus (SBP differs >10 mmHg between inspiration + expiration) — severe asthma, pericardial tamponade

Chest palpation

  • Chest expansion — expect ≥5 cm on inspiration
  • Apex beat (may be impalpable in barrel chest)
  • Tenderness (rib fracture)

🔴 RED — refer ED

  • SpO₂ <88%
  • HR <40 or >130 (resting)
  • BP <90/60 or SBP >200
  • RR <8 or >30
  • Postural drop >15/10 with symptoms
  • Worsening cyanosis (central or peripheral)
  • Severe SOB at rest
  • Acute chest pain (cardiac features)
  • Bilateral fine crackles + SOB (pulmonary oedema)
  • Unilateral calf tenderness (DVT)
  • Stridor (upper airway obstruction)
  • Pulsatile epigastric mass
  • Carotid bruit + neurological symptoms (TIA/stroke risk)

🟠 ORANGE — same-day GP

  • BP grade 2/3 without symptoms
  • New murmur
  • Carotid bruit asymptomatic
  • New peripheral oedema bilateral
  • Persistent cough >3 weeks
  • New onset wheeze
  • ↓ SpO₂ 92–94% with symptoms
  • Unexplained tachycardia or bradycardia

🎯 OSCE pearls

  • Vital signs FIRST — narrate values + interpretation
  • BP: cuff size, position, 5 min rest, 2 readings
  • Pulse 30 sec, rhythm called out
  • SpO₂ technique: nail polish removed, warm hand, 5 sec equilibrate
  • Apex beat at 5th ICS midclavicular line — narrate
  • Don't palpate both carotids simultaneously
  • Auscultate all 4 valve areas in turn
  • Crackles characterisation (early/late, coarse/fine, location)
  • Wheeze localisation (generalised vs focal)

⚠️ Pitfalls

  • "Take BP with cuff over jumper" → falsely high
  • Skin/chest hair rubbing mistaken for crackles
  • Carotid bruit missed → undiagnosed TIA/stroke risk
  • Bilateral pitting oedema dismissed as "just age" → HF missed
  • Pulsatile epigastric mass dismissed as "just abdominal aorta" → AAA missed
